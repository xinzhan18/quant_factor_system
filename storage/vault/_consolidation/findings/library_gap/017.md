# library_gap/017 · F028 dmi_down_ratio 是库内首例 directional sign-aggregated magnitude ratio，整片"directional asymmetry / +DI vs -DI 类"未续探

## Observation

batch_085 admit C003 = `dmi_down_ratio_12` 后 judge 明确陈述:

> "DMI directional pressure asymmetry 在 csi1000 散户震荡市 sign 未翻转 (paper 同向),
> 不需 abs(DMI) magnitude proxy 替代. **库内首次直接的 directional sign-aggregated
> magnitude ratio**."

F028 表达式核心 = `down_count / (down_count + up_count)` 形式，is`Sum(Lt(...) * Greater(|ΔH|,
|ΔL|), 12) / Sum(...)`。这是库内**首次**把"方向性 (Lt/Gt sign) × 幅度 (Abs diff) × 累加 (Sum)"
三要素组合成单一 ratio 的 admit 因子. 在它之前:

- F018 overnight_sign_freq_amount_rank_diff_20: 仅 sign 累加 + amount × rank-diff (无 magnitude
  weighting on sign)
- F010/F011 overnight_return_persistence: signed return Mean (无 directional asymmetry)
- F013 log_amount_weighted_acceptance_20: signed × Log(amount) Mean (单边 directional, 无
  asymmetry)
- F014/F015 amihud variants: signed_return × inv_amount, 但 Mean 形式无 directional asymmetry

**结构性观察**: F028 是 1/28 admit 走 "directional asymmetry" 设计模式. 但 paper-vetted
universe 中该形式族**有 ≥6 个未试 sibling**:

| Sibling 候选 | 几何 | F028 关系 |
|---|---|---|
| `+DI / (+DI + -DI)` (DMI up-ratio) | 镜像 vs F028 down-ratio | sign-mirror, 数学 = 1 - F028 |
| `Bull power / (Bull + Bear power)` (Elder) | (H − EMA_close) / (H − L) 类 | 不同基础几何 |
| `Up-volume Sum / Total volume Sum` 20d | volume 端 directional | num_trades / volume 共族 |
| `(Sum H-prevH for up-day) / (Sum L-prevL for down-day)` 20d | high/low directional asymmetry | F028 同基底但跨字段 |
| `(Up-day amount Mean) / (Down-day amount Mean)` 60d | amount × directional | F002/F012 cluster sibling |
| `RSI 类 = Sum(max(C-Cprev,0)) / Sum(|C-Cprev|)` 14d | up-magnitude vs total magnitude | 经典 paper 形式, 库内零先例 |

batch_088 reject 整族 Chaikin/AD/PVT 是 **first-moment signed × Vol Sum** 形式, 但**没有走
directional asymmetry 路径** (Chaikin 是 EMA-差非比例 ratio). 在 b085 admit F028 后 b088
直接跳过 directional ratio family — 是预算分配未续探导致的缺口, 不是几何死路。
