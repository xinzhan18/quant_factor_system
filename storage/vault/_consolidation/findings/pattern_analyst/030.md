# pattern_analyst/030 · Cov ≈ Mean of product 等价律 (zero-mean stationary return 下数学近似等价)

## Pattern

csi1000 daily 个股 return / overnight gap / intraday body 等序列 cross-section 期望接近 0 (zero-mean stationary), 让 Cov 二阶项消失, 数学上 Cov(X,Y,N) ≈ Mean(X×Y, N). 一旦库内 admit 了 Mean of product 形式 (如 F023 gap_body_magnitude_amount_rd_20), 所有相近窗口的 Cov(同 X, 同 Y, M) 形式 cross-section near_dup, 不可能通过 max_corr hard_gate.

## 证据链

- [[batches/batch_087/judge|batch_087]] overnight_intraday_split C005 `Cov(overnight_gap, intraday_body, 20)` × amount_120: cross-section max_corr=0.927@F023 = `Sub(CsRank(Mean(Mul(Sub($open,Ref($close,1)),Sub($close,$open)),20)),CsRank(Mean($amount,60)))` (>0.9 hard_gate fail)
- 数学推导: csi1000 daily overnight gap mean (cross-section) ≈ 0.0001 + intraday body mean ≈ 0, 让 Cov(o,i,20) = E[oi]_20 - E[o]_20·E[i]_20 ≈ E[oi]_20 = Mean(o×i, 20)
- 与 lessons.md "In-batch denominator equivalence (P024)" 同律家族 — 都是数学近似等价让形式独立但 cross-section 同源

## 升格依据

直接数学证据 + 1 batch 实证 max_corr=0.927 (远超 hard_gate 0.9) + 机制可解释 + 可在 Phase 1 design-time 实施 (AST 自检即可拦截) + 节省 Phase 2 compute 成本 (Cov/Corr 重计算成本高于 Mean-of-product). medium severity 是因为只有 1 batch 直接实证, 但机制清晰且 cost-saving 价值高.

## 建议的 direction 操作

- `overnight_intraday_split` 方向 narrative 加 P028 律 (Cov ≈ Mean of product 等价) 标注, 引用 b087 C005
- Phase 1 generator hard precheck 第 9 条新增: `Cov(return_A, return_B, N)` + 库内含 `Mean(A*B, M∈[N/2, 2N])` → design-time reject
- `anchor_proximity_momentum` / `range_structure` 方向若有 Cov-based candidate 待提, 自检 LHS atom 是否已 admit Mean-of-product 同形
