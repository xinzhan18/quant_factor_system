# pattern_analyst/035 · Conditional truncation 路径 admit 充分条件 — 4 维联合 binarize content ⊥ Barra basis 律

## Pattern

conditional_operator_truncation direction 在 b097-b099 三轮探索完成 family-space 拓扑 mapping: 12 候选累计 1 admit (F029 weak_close_day_rate_20) + 1 reserve (b097/C001 upper_shadow_dominant) + 10 reject. **F029 是 close-position × Lt × 0.2 阈值 × 20d × Mean × 单 condition 的 6 维约束孤立 admissible point**; 任一维度扰动 (window / threshold / aggregate / field / direction / compound) 即跨出 ⊥ basis 区. 本 finding 把这 6 维律 codify, 因为该律影响所有未来 conditional family 探索方向 (gap_acceptance_structure / anchor_proximity / range_structure 内的任何 If/Gt/Lt/Mask candidate).

## 证据链

**✓ PASS 子族 (binarize content ⊥ Barra basis)**:
- [[batches/batch_098/candidates/C004|b098 C004]] `Mean(Lt((c-l)/(h-l), 0.2), 20)` 弱收盘日占比 — **admit F029**: alpha_surv=1.10 Barra-clean + ls_t=3.28 + max_corr=0.32@F006 + incr_ic=-0.002 (borderline incr 但 admit floor 全过)
- [[batches/batch_097/candidates/C001|b097 C001]] `Mean(Gt((H-C)/(H-L), 0.5), 20)` 上影主导日 — **reserve**: alpha_surv=1.07 Barra-clean + ls_t=2.85 (未达 admit floor) + max_corr=0.41@F022 + incr_ic=0.0030

**✗ FAIL 子族 (跨多维度证伪 binarize content ⊥ basis 条件)**:

**维度 1 — sign 方向 (left-tail vs right-tail)**:
- [[batches/batch_098/candidates/C001|b098 C001]] `Mean(Gt((c-l)/(h-l), 0.5), 20)` close 上半段主导日 50% 阈值 (C004 同信号镜像) — reject: alpha_surv=0.50 borderline + vol_20d=16.2 部分 // basis + incr_ic=-0.006. **同 (c-l)/(h-l) 信号底层, 弱端 Lt(.,0.2) ⊥ vol_20d, 上半段 Gt(.,0.5) // vol_20d 部分** — sign 决定同构性, 1:2 alpha_surv 差距 (0.50 vs 1.10).
- [[batches/batch_099/candidates/C004|b099 C004]] open-position Lt 0.2 mirror — reject: alpha_surv=0.15 catastrophic // str_1m basis. **field 切换 (close→open) 即跨入 str_1m basis 吸收区**, mirror 不可推广.

**维度 2 — threshold 阈值 sensitivity**:
- [[batches/batch_098/candidates/C003|b098 C003]] `Mean(Gt(close_pos, 0.8), 20)` 强收盘日 80% 高阈 — hard_gate sign_flip×3: regime instability + 涨停日 (close==high) 强制拉满引入 vol regime sensitivity. **阈值越偏 right-tail, 与 vol_20d basis 同构性差异越显著, 右端 tail (Gt(.,0.8)) 是更深 //**.
- [[batches/batch_099/candidates/C002|b099 C002]] threshold 0.2→0.15 微调 — reject: max_corr=0.82@F029 near_duplicate + incr_ic=-0.0003 NEG. **F029 在 threshold 维度是 quasi-isolated point** (0.15 vs 0.2 0.05 微差即 near_dup).

**维度 3 — field substitution (binarize content 同构 basis)**:
- [[batches/batch_098/candidates/C005|b098 C005]] `Mean(Gt(gap_ret, 0.015), 60)` gap-up 高阈 1.5% — reject: alpha_surv=**0.030** catastrophic + vol_20d_exp=**35.4** 史诗 (b097/C002 阈值 0.5% 同 family 已 alpha_surv=0.13 dead). **T002 gap-event rate family 跨阈值 (0.5%, 1.5%) 结构性 dead**, gap content ≡ vol_20d basis source-level 同构.
- [[batches/batch_097/candidates/C004|b097 C004]] 上涨日占比 (return-sign rate) — reject: alpha_surv=**0.07** 本批最低 + str_1m=5.45. **return-sign ≡ str_1m basis 同构**.
- [[batches/batch_097/candidates/C005|b097 C005]] 低振幅日占比 (range threshold) — reject: alpha_surv=0.22 + vol_20d=**46.8** 本批最高 + max_corr=0.66. **低振幅日占比 ≡ vol_20d basis 反向 direct proxy**.

**维度 4 — window 长度**:
- [[batches/batch_099/candidates/C001|b099 C001]] 60d 长窗弱收盘日 — hard_gate fail OOS 信号完全消失. **F029 family window 上限 < 60d, 20d 是 sweet spot**.

**维度 5 — aggregate wrap (Std vs Mean)**:
- [[batches/batch_099/candidates/C003|b099 C003]] Std-of-binarize 60d — max_corr=0.62@F029 + sign_flip. **Std-of-binarize ≡ Mean-of-binarize 60d 数学等价律** (Var(Bernoulli)=p(1-p) 在 low-rate 区与 p 单调相关).

**维度 6 — compound 复合 condition**:
- [[batches/batch_099/candidates/C006|b099 C006]] F029 base + Gt(amplitude) AND-filter — sign_flip + vol_20d_exp=**45.18** 史诗. **Compound AND 复合两 condition 时 vol_20d-isomorphic condition 主导 Barra basis 同构性**, 即使另一 condition ⊥ basis 也被覆盖.

**维度 7 — partial truncation (mask × raw)**:
- [[batches/batch_097/candidates/C003|b097 C003]] mask × raw turnover — reject: alpha_surv=0.15 + vol_20d=37.4. **partial truncation 保留 magnitude → 100% basis 吸收**. Must full binarize.

**维度 8 — observation gating (If × continuous signal)**:
- [[batches/batch_097/candidates/C006|b097 C006]] If(PV-corr) × continuous momentum — reject: style_r²=**0.71** + str_1m=7.77 + alpha_surv=0.83 paradox. **gating 仅 filter sample 不破 absorption**, momentum 本身 // str_1m basis 不变.

## 升格依据

3 batch 独立 + 12 候选 + 7 个独立失败维度 (sign / threshold / field / window / aggregate / compound / partial / gating) 全 codified + 2 admissible points (F029 + b097/C001 reserve) 反例对照 + 机理可解释 (binarize content 是否同构 Barra style basis 决定 alpha_survival, 同构性受 7 维联合影响) + 实操可执行 (Phase 1 design conditional candidate 必须自检 4 维核心 + 3 维次要). 满足 medium severity (机制清晰 + 多 batch 实证) + lessons 升格 (codify 一类 operator family 的 design checklist).

severity=medium 而非 high 是因为本律范围限于 conditional `If/Gt/Lt/Mask` operator family, 不像 finding/032 (rank-diff axis) 或 finding/033 (T006 atom-agnostic) 那样跨 operator family 普适.
