# L007 Reflection — 聪明钱流量持续性 × 估值过滤

## Initial Hypothesis

股票的amount（成交额 = 价格 × 成交量）自相关性反映了机构大单的资金流向持续性，PE估值过滤后应该产生独立于Barra风格的alpha。

## Mechanism

TsAutoCorr($amount, N) 捕捉smart money accumulation/distribution persistence（机构大单的流入/流出是否持续）。

与现有机制的差异：
- TsAutoCorr($amount) vs TsAutoCorr($volume): amount = price×volume，dollar维度
- TsAutoCorr($amount) vs pv_corr: 自相关vs相关性（持续性vs共变性）
- TsAutoCorr($amount) vs amount × vol-competition: 持续性vs竞争强度

batch_047发现：PE/PS conditioning让dominant_style从turnover_20d转为str_1m/log_circ_cap。这个机制还没有在TsAutoCorr($amount)上测试过。

## Key Findings from batch_065

### All candidates rejected
- **C001**: TsAutoCorr($amount, 20) — max_lib_corr=1.0 vs F001 = DUPLICATE
- **C002**: TsAutoCorr($amount, 10) — weak validation monotonicity (-0.3), no Barra independence
- **C003**: ×PE — ep_ratio dominant (0.227), alpha_surv=0.09 (catastrophic)
- **C004**: ×PE(10d) — vol_20d dominant (0.140), weak mono_val=-0.4
- **C005**: ×PE(30d) — ep_ratio dominant (0.284), decay_ratio=0.577 WEAKENS on holdout
- **C006**: ×PS — str_1m dominant (0.151), near-duplicate (max_lib_corr=0.91)

### Critical insight
**PE/PS conditioning breaks the turnover trap for pv_corr (batch_047) but NOT for TsAutoCorr($amount).**

Same conditioning → different mechanism response. This is a genuine mechanistic distinction:
- pv_corr × PE → dominant_style flips from turnover_20d to str_1m (genuine orthogonality)
- TsAutoCorr($amount) × PE → dominant_style becomes ep_ratio (still Barra-absorbed)

### Conclusion
TsAutoCorr($amount) and TsAutoCorr($volume) are the SAME mechanism class when fundamentals are involved.
The amount field (price × volume) does not denoise microstructure beyond what volume alone captures.

### Lessons learned
1. Solo TsAutoCorr($amount) is novel (batch_050 confirmed NM_001) but amount autocorrelation × PE collapses to styles
2. TsAutoCorr($amount) is essentially TsAutoCorr($volume) — same mechanism class
3. Future attempts on amount field should use non-autocorrelation mechanisms (e.g., Corr($close, $amount) × PE)
