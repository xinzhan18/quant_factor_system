# L015 Reflections: Fundamental-Price Momentum Divergence

## Initial Hypothesis (2026-04-10)

L015 explores PE/PB ratio change vs price change divergence. The signal:
`Sub(CsRank(Div(Ref($pe_ratio,20),$pe_ratio)),CsRank(Div($close,Ref($close,20))))`

This measures: rank(PE_20d_ago / PE_today) - rank(price_today / price_20d_ago)
= "how much did PE ratio improve" minus "how much did price rise"

When PE is improving (falling) but price hasn't caught up, this predicts positive returns.

Novel characteristics:
1. **POSITIVE DIRECTION** — rare! Most of our factors are negative direction
2. **High probe ICIR**: consistently 0.30-0.35 across PE variants (20d, 60d), PB variant
3. **Amount conditioning helps**: ICIR improves to +0.345 with relative-amount filter

Key risks:
- Correlation with Barra ep_ratio (earnings yield = 1/PE) and book_to_price factors
- But the PRICE MOMENTUM interaction (rank difference, not just PE level) may make this orthogonal
- The 20d window makes this a medium-term signal, not just a static value screen
