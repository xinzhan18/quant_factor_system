# L015 Reflections: Fundamental-Price Momentum Divergence

## Initial Hypothesis (2026-04-10)

L015 explores PE/PB ratio change vs price change divergence. The signal:
`Sub(CsRank(Div(Ref($pe_ratio,20),$pe_ratio)),CsRank(Div($close,Ref($close,20))))`

This measures: rank(PE_20d_ago / PE_today) - rank(price_today / price_20d_ago)
= "how much did PE ratio improve" minus "how much did price rise"

When PE is improving (falling) but price hasn't caught up, this predicts positive returns.

Novel characteristics:
1. **POSITIVE DIRECTION** — rare! Most of our factors are negative direction
2. **High probe ICIR**: consistently 0.30-0.35 across PE variants (20d, 60d), PB variant
3. **Amount conditioning helps**: ICIR improves to +0.345 with relative-amount filter

Key risks:
- Correlation with Barra ep_ratio (earnings yield = 1/PE) and book_to_price factors
- But the PRICE MOMENTUM interaction (rank difference, not just PE level) may make this orthogonal
- The 20d window makes this a medium-term signal, not just a static value screen

## Research Insight — batch_099 (2026-04-10)

**PE ratio change decomposition**: PE_today/PE_20d_ago = (Close_today/EPS_today) / (Close_20d_ago/EPS_20d_ago) = (Close_today/Close_20d_ago) × (EPS_20d_ago/EPS_today)

So: Div(Ref($pe_ratio,20),$pe_ratio) = price_ratio × inverse_eps_ratio

The CsRank of this compound ratio reflects BOTH:
1. Stocks where earnings improved (EPS_today > EPS_20d_ago) — genuine alpha
2. Stocks where price fell (making PE look cheaper) — value trap

Subtracting price momentum: `Sub(CsRank(Div(Ref($pe_ratio,20),$pe_ratio)), CsRank(Div($close,Ref($close,20))))` does NOT cleanly isolate earnings improvement because the price ratio is embedded inside the PE change, not additive.

**Why 60d window is better**: At 60d, the lookback spans ~3 months (quarterly earnings cycle). True earnings improvement (quarterly EPS beats) is more likely to drive PE changes at this horizon vs short-term price noise. The 60d signal has smaller sign flip (barra_res_icir=-0.184) because it better separates genuine fundamental improvement from price-driven PE changes.

**The critical next test**: Pure EPS proxy = `Div(Div($close,$pe_ratio),Div(Ref($close,20),Ref($pe_ratio,20)))` = EPS_today / EPS_20d_ago. This is price-REMOVED earnings change. If this has positive direction IC AND positive barra_res_icir, it confirms the earnings improvement mechanism is real and barra-clean. If direction is negative or barra_res_icir is negative, then earnings momentum does not predict returns in this universe.
